Programs

Practical, real-world learning in money, careers, and the conversations that decide both. Three curricula are developed and ready to deliver with partners; two more programs are on the way.

Curriculum developed

Personal Finance 101

Financial literacy for students and young adults

Most people learn about money by making expensive mistakes. Personal Finance 101 is practical education that covers the decisions young people will face first, in language they can use right away.

  • Banking and how accounts work
  • Budgeting that survives real life
  • Credit: building it and protecting it
  • Debt management
  • Investing fundamentals
  • Retirement planning, started early
  • Financial decision-making
Curriculum developed

Career Readiness LaunchPad

Career preparation and professional skills

The first job search is the hardest one. The Career Readiness LaunchPad teaches the skills that get a student noticed, remembered, and hired, and it is designed to be delivered alongside employers and volunteer professionals.

  • Resumes that get read
  • LinkedIn profiles
  • Interviewing
  • Professional communication
  • Networking
  • Job-search strategy
Curriculum developed

Interview and Mock Interview Readiness

Structured practice with professional feedback

Confidence in an interview comes from having done it before. This program pairs structured preparation with one-on-one mock interviews led by professionals, honest feedback, and a career-development action plan each student takes with them.

  • Interview preparation
  • One-on-one mock interviews
  • Professional feedback
  • Career-development action plans
Planned

Mentorship and Networking

In development

Connecting students and young adults with experienced professionals who can provide guidance, career exposure, and constructive support. If you are a professional who would like to mentor, tell us: the mentors we meet now will shape how this program is built.

Exploring

Athletics and Wellness

Potential future program

We are exploring youth-development opportunities through sports, wellness, teamwork, and athletic involvement. Student-athletes are a core audience for everything we do, and athletics teaches discipline, resilience, and teamwork that carry into every career.

How a program comes to your students

Every program is delivered through a school, college, or community partner. Here is what that looks like.

  1. Request a workshop

    Tell us about your students, the program you are interested in, and the timing that works for you.

  2. Plan it together

    We adapt the curriculum outline to your group, your schedule, and the professionals we can bring with us.

  3. Deliver and follow up

    Sessions are run with your staff present, and students leave with materials and an action plan.
