Resources

Three developed curriculum outlines, available to schools, colleges, and partners who want to see exactly what a program covers before bringing it to their students.

Curriculum outlines

Request any outline by email and we will send it along with a note on how we tailor it.

Request an outline

  • Personal Finance 101

    Banking, budgeting, credit, debt management, investments, retirement planning, and financial decision-making. Written for students and young adults who are managing money for the first time.

  • Career Readiness LaunchPad

    Resumes, LinkedIn profiles, interviewing, professional communication, networking, and job-search skills, designed to be delivered with employers and volunteer professionals.

  • Interview and Mock Interview Readiness

    Structured interview preparation, one-on-one practice, professional feedback, and career-development action plans. Includes the format for a mock-interview day with volunteer professionals.
