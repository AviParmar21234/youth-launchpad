A bridge between the classroom and everything after it.

The Youth LaunchPad Foundation empowers young people through education, mentorship, athletics, and career-readiness programs that build confidence, strengthen communities, and prepare students for lifelong success.

What we believe

Our mission

The Youth LaunchPad Foundation empowers young people through education, mentorship, athletics, and career-readiness programs that build confidence, strengthen communities, and prepare students for lifelong success.

Our vision

A future where every young person has the knowledge, confidence, and opportunities to achieve financial independence and long-term success.

Our story

The Youth LaunchPad Foundation was founded on a simple belief: every kid deserves opportunities to learn and grow beyond the classroom. Access to practical financial knowledge, career preparation, trusted mentors, and real-world opportunities can help kids build confidence, make informed decisions, and prepare for their futures.

We are starting where we have roots, in Florida and Pennsylvania, with three developed curriculum outlines and a growing circle of schools, employers, and community partners. The goal is a model that works well enough to scale to communities nationwide.

Who we serve, and where.

Our programs are for students, student-athletes, and young adults who can benefit from real-world financial education, career preparation, mentorship, and life-skills development.

  • FloridaIncorporated in Florida on June 10, 2026, and where our first school and college partnerships are taking shape.
  • PennsylvaniaOur founder’s personal connections to Pennsylvania’s educational community are where this began.

Long term, we are developing a scalable model that can eventually expand to communities nationwide.

Leadership

The Foundation is organized for charitable and educational purposes and is intended to operate under board governance.

Michael Liebert

Founder & Executive Director

Michael Liebert is the founder of The Youth LaunchPad Foundation, Inc., an organization focused on expanding access to practical financial education, career readiness, mentorship, and opportunities for young people.

With personal connections to Pennsylvania’s educational community, Michael is working to establish partnerships that connect students with the skills, guidance, and professional exposure needed to prepare for their futures.

The organization

The Youth LaunchPad Foundation, Inc. was incorporated in Florida on June 10, 2026 (Florida document number N26000008380). It is organized for charitable and educational purposes.

We are a new organization and we say so plainly. Programs described on this site are in development, our board of directors is being formed, and we will publish financial and program results here only once they are supported by records.
