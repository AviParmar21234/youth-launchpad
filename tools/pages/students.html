The things nobody teaches you, taught.

How to budget your first paycheck. What a credit score actually is. How to write a resume when you have never had a job, and what to say when an interviewer asks about your weaknesses. Youth LaunchPad programs are built around the questions students really have.

What you will walk away with

  • A budget you can keep

    Personal Finance 101 covers banking, budgeting, credit, debt, investing, and retirement, in plain language, with decisions you will face in the next few years, not the next few decades.

    Personal Finance 101

  • A resume and a LinkedIn profile that work

    The Career Readiness LaunchPad teaches how to present what you have done, communicate like a professional, build a network, and run a job search.

    Career Readiness LaunchPad

  • Interview practice with real professionals

    Mock interviews, one on one, with honest feedback and an action plan you keep. The first real interview will not be your first interview.

    Interview Readiness

  • People in your corner

    Our mentorship program is in development. When it launches, it will connect you with professionals who can offer guidance, career exposure, and support.

    Mentorship and networking

Student-athletes: your schedule is the point, not the problem.

Practice, travel, and competition leave little room for anything else, and that is exactly when career and money skills matter most. Our programs are designed to fit around a team’s calendar and to speak to the discipline athletes already have.

How to join a program

Programs are delivered through schools, colleges, teams, and community organizations, so the fastest route is through a teacher, coach, counselor, or advisor. Point them to our workshop request form, or contact us and we will reach out to your school.
