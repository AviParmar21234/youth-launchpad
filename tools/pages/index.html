For students, student-athletes, and young adults who want more than a classroom can give them.

Our programs are for anyone who can benefit from real-world financial education, career preparation, mentorship, and life-skills development. We reach them through the schools, colleges, and community organizations they already belong to.

  • FloridaIncorporated here, and where our first school and college partnerships are taking shape.
  • PennsylvaniaWhere our founder’s connections to the educational community began.

Long term, we are building a model that can scale to communities nationwide.

Questions we hear most.

Who does Youth LaunchPad serve?

Students, student-athletes, and young adults are the intended audiences for our developing programs. We reach them through schools, colleges, and community organizations.

Where are you located?

Youth LaunchPad is incorporated in Florida, with an initial geographic focus on Florida and Pennsylvania.

How can a school get involved?

Submit a workshop request and we will follow up to explore workshops, programs, and partnership opportunities that fit your students.

Are programs free?

Program availability, eligibility, and pricing have not yet been finalized. We will publish details here as each program launches.
