Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

The Youth LaunchPad Foundation, Inc. (“Youth LaunchPad,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through youthlaunchpad.org, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, organization, role, and the contents of your message. We do not ask for, and you should not send, financial account numbers, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit, for security and to keep the site running.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about programs, partnerships, volunteering, or sponsorship.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Children and students

This website is intended for adults: educators, partners, volunteers, parents, and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this site. Students interested in our programs should ask a teacher, coach, counselor, or parent to contact us on their behalf.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future, we will update this policy to describe what is collected.

Third-party services

Fonts on this site are served by Google Fonts, which may record requests to deliver the font files. Form submissions may be processed by a form-handling service acting on our behalf; such a provider is permitted to use your information only to deliver your message to us.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you have submitted by emailing info@youthlaunchpad.org. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information through this site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Foundation grows. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@youthlaunchpad.org.
