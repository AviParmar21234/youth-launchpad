Schools and partners

Every Youth LaunchPad program reaches students through a partner. Schools give us the classroom, employers give us the professionals, and community organizations give us the trust. Here is how each one works.

Schools and universities

High schools, colleges, universities, and educational organizations.

We collaborate with schools to explore financial-literacy workshops, career-preparation programming, mock interviews, and mentorship opportunities for students. Each curriculum is a developed outline we adapt to your students, your schedule, and your staff.

A partnership can start as small as a single workshop. Tell us about your students and we will propose a format.

Request a workshop

Corporate partners

Employers who want their people in front of the next generation.

Collaboration opportunities include employee volunteering, professional speakers, career-exposure sessions, mock interviews, sponsorships, and in-kind contributions.

Prospective sponsors can support curriculum development, educational materials, volunteer engagement, and future workshops. We will work with you on recognition that fits your company.

  • Send professionals to run mock interviews or speak to a class
  • Host a career-exposure session at your workplace
  • Sponsor a program, a workshop series, or curriculum materials
  • Contribute in kind: space, materials, or expertise

Start a sponsorship conversation

Community organizations

The groups young people already trust.

We welcome conversations with Greek-life organizations, student clubs, athletic organizations, alumni associations, nonprofits, and community groups. If you bring young people together, we can bring a program to them.

How a partnership works

  1. Tell us about your group

    Use the form that fits you best. We ask only what we need to propose something useful.

  2. We propose a format

    A workshop, a series, a mock-interview day, a speaker session, or a pilot. We adapt the curriculum to your students and schedule.

  3. We deliver, then grow it

    Sessions run with your staff present. What works becomes the next program, built with you.
