Questions and answers

If yours is not here, ask us.

Who does Youth LaunchPad serve?

Students, student-athletes, and young adults are the intended audiences for our developing programs. We reach them through schools, colleges, teams, and community organizations.

Where are you located?

Youth LaunchPad is incorporated in Florida, with an initial geographic focus on Florida and Pennsylvania. Our long-term goal is a model that can expand to communities nationwide.

How can schools become involved?

Schools can submit a workshop request to explore future workshops, educational programs, and partnership opportunities. A single workshop is a fine place to start.

Are programs free?

Program availability, eligibility, and pricing have not yet been finalized. We will publish details as each program launches.

What programs do you offer?

Three curricula are developed: Personal Finance 101, the Career Readiness LaunchPad, and Interview and Mock Interview Readiness. A mentorship and networking program is planned, and we are exploring athletics and wellness. See Programs.

How can a company get involved?

Employee volunteering, professional speakers, career-exposure sessions, mock interviews, sponsorships, and in-kind contributions. Start with a sponsorship inquiry or a partner request.

Can I volunteer or mentor?

Yes. Professionals can help with resumes, interviews, financial education, career guidance, and community initiatives. Tell us about yourself on the volunteer form.

Can I donate?

We are not accepting online donations yet. The Foundation is a Florida nonprofit corporation, and we will open giving options once the appropriate legal and operational steps are complete. In the meantime, sponsorship conversations are welcome.

Are you a registered 501(c)(3)?

The Youth LaunchPad Foundation, Inc. was incorporated in Florida on June 10, 2026, and is organized for charitable and educational purposes. We will update this page when our tax-exempt status is confirmed. Until then, please do not treat contributions as tax-deductible.
